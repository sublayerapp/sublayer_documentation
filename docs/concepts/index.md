# Core Concepts
Explore the foundational components of the Sublayer framework: Generators, Actions, and Agents. Updated to reflect the latest enhancements and best practices.

## Table of Contents
- [Generators](#generators)
- [Actions](#actions)
- [Agents](#agents)

---

## Generators
Generators are responsible for creating specific outputs based on given inputs. They focus on one generation task without complex decision-making.
- [Learn more about Generators](/docs/concepts/generators)

## Actions
Actions perform operations to produce input for a Generator or act on generator outputs. They are essential to external communications and interactions.
- [Learn more about Actions](/docs/concepts/actions)

## Agents
Agents are autonomous entities performing specific tasks or monitoring systems based on defined triggers and goals.
- [Learn more about Agents](/docs/concepts/agents)
